How Scudi builds a slip

Reads the sharpest pricesOver ten seasons the market's own probabilities beat our model every year, so every chance on this page comes from them.

Searches every combinationResult, double chance and goals picks for every match: it keeps the one set with the highest chance that still reaches your multiplier.

Checks it against SNAIYou type the prices SNAI shows for the legs. Scudi recomputes the odds, the bonus and what the slip really costs, and swaps a leg if the target is missed.

How many legs?

Serie A at 25x: chance of landing by number of legs, and the average return per €1 with SNAI's bonus.

Using all ten matches costs about ten cents per euro against the best six or seven. The top 5 slip chooses its ten from about 45 matches, so it does much better: 1 in 33 and €0.94. At SNAI's own prices these returns will be lower if its margins are wider than Bet365's.

The exam it had to pass

Pass marks written down first, then run once on two seasons never used in development (2024-25 and 2025-26, 3,503 matches).

A wider pool helps a little

Same 562 weekends, best ten at 25x, chance of landing and return per €1 with the bonus:

Bet365's chances are as honest in Serie B, the Championship and the other second divisions (worst gap 2.3 points in the slip band). The gain is small because the top-5 weekend already offers 45 matches; the wider pool matters most on thin weekends.

New markets, checked on 21 seasons

Multigol, team goals, combos, 1X2 handicap and first-half picks, all from the same score matrix. A small correction fitted on 2005-06 to 2016-17, then checked untouched on 2017-18 to 2023-24 and once more on 2024-25 and 2025-26 (125,544 matches, 17 leagues). Only the types that passed are offered, and only from 30% up, where they were checked.

Pass mark: promised against happened within 2.5 points (4 points for bands of 150-499 picks), in every band of 30-55%, 55-85% and 85-97%. Left out: every pick that needs a team not to score (too kind by 4-8 points, as "not both score"), "exactly by one" handicaps and first-half "home win" and "both score". SNAI's own prices for these markets exist in no free file: the site starts from a typical margin (9% multigol, 11% combo, 8.5% the others) and learns SNAI's real ones from the pages you paste.
