How Scudi builds a slip

Reads the sharpest pricesOver ten seasons the market's own probabilities beat our model every year, so every chance on this page comes from them.

Searches every combinationResult, double chance and goals picks for every match: it keeps the one set with the highest chance that still reaches your multiplier.

Checks it against SNAIYou type the prices SNAI shows for the legs. Scudi recomputes the odds, the bonus and what the slip really costs, and swaps a leg if the target is missed.
